# Audio attributions

Ambient sounds used in the Meditation mode (F29), bundled at
`LumiApp/Resources/Audio/`. Both source recordings are public domain, so no
attribution is legally required, but it's recorded here for traceability.

## ambient-rain.m4a

- Source: [File:Rain against the window.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain_against_the_window.ogg), Wikimedia Commons
- Original author: cori (via pdsounds.org)
- License: Public domain
- Change made: re-encoded from Ogg Vorbis to AAC/m4a (iOS's AVFoundation
  does not decode Ogg Vorbis), mono, 96 kbps. Full ~82s length kept, looped
  in-app.

## ambient-ocean.m4a

- Source: [File:Waves.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Waves.ogg), Wikimedia Commons
- Original author: Dsw4 (recorded autumn 2008, Lake Ontario shore, Webster NY)
- License: Public domain
- Change made: re-encoded from Ogg Vorbis to AAC/m4a, trimmed to a 60s
  segment (skipping the first 5s), mono, 96 kbps. Looped in-app.
